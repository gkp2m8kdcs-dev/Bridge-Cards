1. Overview

Bridge Cards ("we", "our", "the app") is a single-player card-based puzzle game developed for iOS. Privacy is a core design principle: the app runs entirely on your device, does not require an account, and does not transmit gameplay data to our servers or to any third party.

This Privacy Policy explains what information, if any, is processed when you use Bridge Cards and the choices you have. By using the app, you agree to the practices described below.

2. Data We Collect

We collect no personal data from you. In particular:

  • Account data: We do not require or create accounts. There is no sign-up, login, or profile.
  • Gameplay data: Scores, times, moves, and workshop progress are stored locally on your device using standard iOS storage. They never leave the device.
  • Photos: Photos you capture or import in the Gallery tab are stored locally in the app's private container. We do not upload them to any server.
  • Diagnostics: We do not embed crash reporting, analytics, or telemetry SDKs. We do not receive device identifiers, advertising IDs, or usage events.
  • Network: The app makes no outbound network requests for gameplay. Any updates you receive come exclusively through the App Store.

3. Permissions We Request

Bridge Cards only requests the operating-system permissions that are strictly necessary for the feature you choose to use. iOS will prompt you the first time each permission is needed, and you can revoke them at any time in Settings → Privacy & Security.

  • Camera — used only when you tap "Capture" in the Gallery tab to take a scenery photo inside the app. The captured image is saved to the app's local storage. We do not stream, upload, or process camera frames on a server.
  • Photo Library (Read) — used only when you tap "Import" in the Gallery tab to pick an existing image. Imported images are copied into the app's local storage; we do not retain access to the rest of your library.
  • Photo Library (Write / Add) — used only when you explicitly choose to save a finished bridge screenshot to your photo library. We never modify or delete photos without your action.

If you decline a permission, the related feature is disabled but the rest of the app continues to work.

4. Third-Party Services

Bridge Cards does not integrate any third-party advertising, analytics, social, or payment SDKs. There are no third parties with access to your data through the app. All rendering is done on-device with Apple frameworks (SwiftUI, UIKit, and SF Symbols).

5. Children's Privacy

The app is suitable for all ages and does not collect any personal information from anyone, including children under 13 (or the applicable age of digital consent in your country). Because there is no data collection, no parental verification is required.

6. Your Rights and Choices

Because we do not collect personal data, most data-subject rights are not applicable. You can still:

  • Delete all locally stored gameplay records via Settings → Reset Data in the app.
  • Delete imported or captured photos individually from the Gallery tab.
  • Uninstall the app at any time to remove all local data and photos from your device.
  • Revoke camera or photo-library permissions at any time in iOS Settings.

7. Changes to This Policy

If we ever change this policy — for example, to add a clearly optional online feature — we will update the "Last updated" date above and, where required, surface a notice in the app or on this page before the change takes effect. Continued use of the app after the effective date means you accept the revised policy.
