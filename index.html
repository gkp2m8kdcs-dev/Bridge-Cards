Engineer your arch, one swap at a time

Each column holds a stack of face-up and face-down cards. The top card's rank decides how tall that support pillar reaches. Same-color runs become structural beams that visibly tie your bridge together.

Frequently asked questions

Quick answers about gameplay, accounts, and your data.

Do I need an account to play? ▾

No. Bridge Cards is fully offline and account-free. Open the app and start building.

Does the app show ads or track me? ▾

Never. There are no ads, no analytics SDKs, and no third-party trackers of any kind.

How do I save or share a photo? ▾

Open the Gallery tab, capture a scene with the camera or import from your photo library. The image stays on your device until you choose to delete it from the app or your system settings.

Can I reset my progress? ▾

Yes. Settings offers a one-tap data reset for records and gallery photos. This action cannot be undone.

Which devices are supported? ▾

Bridge Cards runs on iPhone with iOS 16 or later. It is built natively with SwiftUI.
