Need help with Bridge Cards, found a bug, or just want to share feedback? Our small team reads every message. Pick the channel that suits you below.

Best for bug reports, account-style questions, and feature requests. We typically reply within 2 business days.

In-app help

Open Bridge Cards, go to Settings → Help to send a report that includes your device model and app version (no personal data is attached).

Gameplay tips

Tap a column to select its top card, then tap an adjacent column to swap. Build a smooth arch with the peak in the middle to maximize your stability score.

Reset progress

Open Settings → Reset Data in the app to clear your local records and gallery photos. The action cannot be undone.

Frequently asked questions

Quick answers before you write in.

Where is my data stored? ▾

Everything stays on your device. Records and photos live in the app's private container, and they are removed when you uninstall Bridge Cards or use Settings → Reset Data.

The app feels stuck on a column. What should I do? ▾

Some columns can have no valid swap that improves your arch. Tap the same column again to deselect, then try a different pair. Stability rewards a smooth arch shape, not random swaps.

How do I unlock the next workshop? ▾

Send the car across any bridge with a stability score that meets or exceeds the workshop's target. The next workshop will then be available in the Levels tab.

Can I play offline? ▾

Yes. Bridge Cards is fully offline. You don't need Wi-Fi or mobile data to play.

Is the app translated into other languages? ▾

The first release ships in English. Additional languages may be added in future updates.
